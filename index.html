Talent Acquisition
Rebuilding the Technical Hiring Framework
Quvia · 2023–Present
Situation
~35% rejection from panels on profiles that cleared HR screening. Engineering leads frustrated.
Action
6-month diagnostic, role definition templates, competency scorecards, panel calibration sessions.
Rejection rate dropped 25% in 2 quarters. Engineering now co-owns role design with HR.
