Talent Acquisition
Rebuilding the Technical Hiring Framework
Quvia (Espace Networks) · 2023–Present
Situation
~35% candidate rejection from panels on profiles that had cleared HR screening. Engineering leads frustrated with wasted interview rounds.
Action
Diagnosed 6 months of rejection feedback. Co-created role definition templates. Introduced competency-based scorecards and structured panel calibration sessions.
Rejection rate dropped 25% in 2 quarters. Engineering now proactively partners with HR on role design.
Hiring Efficiency
Cutting Time-to-Hire from 35 to 24 Days
Quvia (Espace Networks) · 2023–Present
Situation
35+ day average TTH. Top candidates accepting competing offers. Sourcing over-dependent on low-quality job board pipelines.
Action
Relaunched referral program. Built talent pipelines for recurring roles. Implemented parallel scheduling. Created a warm candidate database.
TTH: 35→24 days. Referral hires: <10%→30%+. Acceptance: 70%→88%. CPH down 20%.
People Analytics
Attrition Intelligence Program
Quvia (Espace Networks) · 2024
Situation
Anecdotal awareness of attrition but no structured data. Two key engineers left citing "lack of growth clarity" — no early warning system.
Action
Redesigned exit interview template. Built attrition dashboard segmented by team, tenure, reason. Launched quarterly People Insights reports to leadership.
Two targeted retention interventions launched. Transparent L&D path + manager effectiveness program implemented.
Vendor Management
HR Vendor Cost Negotiation
Quvia (Espace Networks) · 2024
Situation
HR vendor subscriptions (Naukri, SpringVerify, certification partners) on legacy pricing. No structured renegotiation process existed.
Action
Became single POC for all HR vendors. Conducted usage audits, benchmarked market rates, led structured contract negotiations, introduced SLA tracking.
